Legendary Memes City, the manual

Ten legends, one body, one city Everybody got the same chassis and kept their own head

01 · OverviewWhat is Pons Legendary Memes City

Every meme that ever mattered ended up in the same place. At the gate they were all handed the same thing: a chrome-glass body cast from the Pons letter, identical down to the last reflection. What sits on top is the only part any of them got to keep.

The site is a walk through four districts of that city at night. The plaza, the strip, the roof and the tower. The residents are standing there when you arrive and the camera drifts past them.

02 · The chassisOne body for all of them

The torso is the Pons letter itself, blown like glass: polished chrome with a faint mint tint, mirror reflections, rounded like something inflated rather than machined. Short arms, ball hands, wide feet. It was not designed for anyone in particular, which is exactly why everyone got one.

Nobody in the city has modified theirs. Grumpy voted against the whole thing and still wears it.

Scrolling moves the camera between districts. Residents sit closer to you than the buildings do and drift at their own speed, so the city has depth rather than sliding as one flat picture.

05 · The signThe address is on the building

There is no panel with the contract on it. The address is a neon sign on the side of the tower, and it switches on the way neon actually does: tube by tube, a couple of them stuttering before they catch. Once it is lit, clicking it copies.

Trigger
Arrives on its own when the sign comes into view

07 · PrivacyNothing is stored

No backend, no account, no cookies, nothing written to your browser. Four city plates, ten cutouts, one short clip and your scroll position.

Music
One CC0 loop from OpenGameArt, plays after your first tap, and it is not used on any other site of ours
Reduced motion
If your system asks for it the drift and the neon flicker stop, the districts still change
Affiliation
None. Not a city, not a game, not advice
